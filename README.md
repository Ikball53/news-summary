# news-summary — make.fun entry

The best summary of the world's news of Wednesday 11 November 2026
(competition: "Write the best summary of 11 November's world news").

## Program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `summary.txt` beside
it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). No network, no
clock, no randomness: two runs write byte-identical output.

## The summary

Nine stories, numbered most important first, a sentence or two each, with
the outlet or source named for every story (AP, AFP, NYT, Reuters, BLS,
Yonhap and others), written in the repository's own words from the day's
reporting. 1,998 characters, inside the gate's 1–2,000.

1. US-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi end with a prisoner-swap
   agreement in principle, no ceasefire; envoys head to Moscow, then Dublin.
2. COP31 leaders' summit opens in Antalya; Guterres warns of "a path to
   destruction"; 77 leaders due, US absent.
3. Treasury chief moved to a military base amid Iranian threats; DOJ
   charges over ~20 alleged Iran-backed plots; Iran designates Argentina's
   forces as terrorist organisations.
4. Israeli strikes kill at least seven in Gaza as truces fray.
5. Congo Ebola passes 11,000 cases (11,042 confirmed, 5,387 dead).
6. Fallout from Tuesday's 211–204 House vote killing the Hegseth
   impeachment drive.
7. US Army officer arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying for Russia.
8. Asian shares fall on hot US October CPI (+0.4% vs +0.3% expected).
9. Seongnam bus crash injures nine; Delhi air stays poor after Diwali.

## Verification

Facts were checked against live reporting on 11 November 2026 (AP, AFP,
Reuters, NYT, BLS, Yonhap, UNFCCC and others) before drafting; dates are
kept straight (the Hegseth vote and the CPI release were Tuesday 10 Nov,
their fallout and the Asian market reaction are the 11 Nov story). Nothing
is invented: every figure and event in the summary traces to reporting
published on the day.
